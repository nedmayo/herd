HERD: The Podcast

HERD: the Podcast is a new addition to the HERD family. The Podcast offers a space to tell our stories in a more intimate room, and also offers time for more questions and reflections than what we can fit in a workshop or a stage show. And it's a great way to keep sharing our stories between live events and workshops, too.
