HERD: OUTSIDE

April 22, 2026 · Earth Day · 7:00pm

Location: The Parkway

This Earth Day, our theme honors the power of being OUTSIDE—outside the norm, outside the script, outside the noise. It’s where the wild things are, and where women have always gone to find clarity, courage, and connection. We’ve all been outsiders—standing at the threshold, peering through windows into worlds we didn’t quite belong to. OUTSIDE brings together stories of exclusion, inclusion, reinvention, and the unexpected beauty of finding your own way in. OUTSIDE celebrates every place we step beyond the familiar—into the woods, onto the ledge, past our fears, or outside the lines someone else drew for us. From wild adventures to moments of feeling on the fringe, these stories remind us how much we grow when we step out into the open.
